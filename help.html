FAQ

How do I submit a maintenance request?

Go to the "Requests" page, fill out the form, and click "Create request".

Can I cancel a request?

Yes — use the "Cancel" button on the request to stop it from moving forward.

How do I get notifications?

Notifications appear in the bell icon when a request status changes or a due date is near.
